## Interpretation

The model performs better at identifying FAKE videos than REAL videos. The relatively low precision and F1-score for the REAL class indicate that the model needs further improvement.

The ROC-AUC of 0.6689 indicates modest discrimination between the two classes. The results should be treated as an initial experimental baseline, not evidence of reliable real-world deepfake detection.

The evaluation used the held-out test split, which was not used for training or checkpoint selection.
